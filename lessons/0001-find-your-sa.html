Find your Sa

Today's win: you pick a home note that suits your voice and hold it in tune for three seconds.

Four ideas, then we sing

1. Pitch is how high or low a sound is. It is measured in hertz (Hz), the number of vibrations per second. A note at 220 Hz doubled to 440 Hz sounds like the same note, only higher. That doubling is called an octave.

2. Sa is your home note, and you choose it. In Indian music Sa is not a fixed pitch: the performer sets it wherever suits the voice, and every other swara is placed relative to it (Clarke, ch. 1). The Western letters C, D, E are fixed pitches. So "my Sa is C♯" just means you chose to sit your home on C♯ (Stanford CCRMA).

3. A drone keeps home in your ears. The tanpura or shruti box plays Sa and its fifth, Pa, all the time, and beginners are told to listen to it carefully and pick a Sa that lets them sing comfortably across about two octaves (karnatik.com beginners guide).

4. "In tune" has a number. One step between neighbouring notes is 100 cents. Swara Studio's green zone is 25 cents either side of the note: a quarter of a step.

Memory hack

Think of Sa as the tonic you return to, like home in a story. Do re mi works the same way: "do" can sit on any letter, which is why it is called movable do.

Step 1 · Try candidate Sa notes

Tap Play, hum along very softly on "mm". Step down until humming starts to feel growly or breathy, then step back up one or two. Pick a note you could hum for a minute without effort.

C3131 Hz

Lower voices often land around C3 to E3, higher voices around G3 to C4. Trust your comfort, not the letter.

Step 2 · Sit inside the drone

  1. Put earphones in and start the drone.
  2. Breathe in, and sing a soft, open "aa" on Sa for one full breath. Do this three times.
  3. On the fourth breath, slide slowly up and back down to Sa, like a siren. Notice the moment it "locks" with the drone: the buzz smooths out.
Memory hack

Sa up to Pa is the leap on the second "twinkle" in Twinkle, Twinkle. Sing that tune starting on your Sa and the second "twinkle" lands on Pa.

Step 3 · Score it

Open Swara Studio, choose Sing → Hold Sa. On the standalone app, sing live and hold the line in the green band for 3 seconds. Inside Claude, record 5 seconds of "aa" with your phone's recorder and upload it.

This is gate 1 of Stage 1: three seconds in the green, on three different days. The app counts the days for you.
