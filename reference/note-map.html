Note map

The 12 notes from Sa to Ni, in Hindustani, Carnatic, movable do and Western names. Western letters depend on where your Sa sits, so pick it below.

StepsSwaraHindustaniCarnaticDo re miLetter

Brass rows are Sa and Pa, which never change. A line under a name means komal (flat); a line over it means tivra (sharp). Carnatic names follow Stanford CCRMA's table; some positions have two Carnatic names (R2 is also G1, G2 is also R3, D2 is also N1, N2 is also D3), and which one is used depends on the raga.

Marks follow NIOS Hindustani theory, ch. 5. Carnatic books vary: karnatik.com writes the middle octave in lowercase (s r g), the upper octave in capitals (S R G) and the lower octave with a dot after the note (s.).

Western intervals, for later

Each swara is also a named distance from Sa: Re is a major 2nd, Ga a major 3rd, Ma a perfect 4th, Pa a perfect 5th, Dha a major 6th, Ni a major 7th, and taar Sa an octave. Komal notes are the minor versions; tivra Ma is the augmented 4th.
